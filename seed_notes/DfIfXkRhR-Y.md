## Transkrypcja

Claude is the world's most convincing yes man. Ask why your idea is good and it'll defend it.
Ask why it's bad and it'll tear the same idea apart. But there's a skill that makes it challenge
your idea instead of just following your lead. It's called LLM Council built by Ole Lehmann
and instead of getting one opinion you get five different perspectives.
First, install the skill, then give Claude a decision you're stuck on and say ask the
council. One advisor looks for flaws, another questions what you're actually trying to
solve. A third looks for opportunities you've missed. The fourth gets no personal background
about you so it approaches the question with fresh eyes. And the fifth focuses on what you
should actually do next. Now here's the trick. Their answers get shuffled and passed to five
reviewers without revealing which advisor wrote each one. Then a final chairman combines
the arguments into one recommendation and a clear next step. You can see where the
reasoning holds up and where the advisors disagree. Try it with should I add this
feature or which of these ideas should I work on first? Give it your options, your
budget and what you're trying to achieve. If you want the skill and setup guide
just follow me and comment council and I'll send them over.
